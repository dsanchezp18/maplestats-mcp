# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, in short form.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, in English and in French, whatever their age, background,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Report data problems with the source, the exact call and what you expected,
  so they can be fixed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and personal or
  political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not follow them, and may ban a contributor for behaviour judged
inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private
[report a vulnerability](https://github.com/dsanchezp18/maplestats-mcp/security/advisories/new)
form, or open an issue that asks for private contact without giving details.
Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
