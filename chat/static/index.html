MapleStats Chat

Ask about Canadian open data. Claude Haiku, a small AI model, looks the answer up live through the MapleStats MCP server. Under each answer: a chart and a table of the data the server returned, and its sources.

StatCan's data service is temporarily refusing automated requests; some answers may be unavailable or come from other sources.

Le service de données de Statistique Canada refuse temporairement les requêtes automatisées ; certaines réponses peuvent être indisponibles ou provenir d'autres sources.

Before you ask
  • AI answers can be wrong. The model can misread a table, pick the wrong series or period, or miscalculate. Check every figure against the source listed under the answer before you use it.
  • Not an official source. MapleStats is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Statistics Canada, the Bank of Canada or any other government agency; their published releases are the authoritative figures.
  • Not advice. Answers are for information only, not financial, legal, tax or investment advice.
  • Privacy. Your questions are sent to Anthropic, the model provider, to be answered. Do not enter names, contact details or other personal or confidential information.
  • A limited demo. Questions per hour and per day are capped, and an answer can stop early when a question needs many lookups. It may be unavailable or change without notice.
